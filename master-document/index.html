Puca dash — ignition, mph, range, in-app nav

Live rider display (phone on the magnetic mount). Open full screen: https://www.skeletkey.com/app/

  • Ignition — always starts OFF. IGN toggles a BLE relay (ESP32) on the enable/contactor coil; keep a physical key or e-stop in series.
  • mph — GPS speedometer with trip, max, avg, time, heading, altitude; mph/km/h toggle. Odo saved in the browser.
  • Range — estimated miles + pack % on screen (BMS will drive this on the ~8.5 kWh / ~60 lb module). FarDriver app is for controller tuning.
  • Navigate — OpenStreetMap stays in this view. Search a destination, tap Navigate for an OSRM route (no new tab).

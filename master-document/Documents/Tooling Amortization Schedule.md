# Assembly fixture & test NRE schedule
**Project:** Puca 10,000 units/year  
**This is not a frame-die program.** SkeletKey sources folding frames complete.  
**Updated:** 2026-08-21

### 1. What this money is for

| Bucket | Purpose | NRE (order of magnitude) | Amortization |
| :--- | :--- | ---: | :--- |
| Assembly jigs | Dropout, fold, deck, pack slide | $40,000 | First 5,000 units |
| Incoming gauges | Frame/headset/pack IQC | $20,000 | First 5,000 units |
| EOL test | Hipot, short roller/dyno, spray | $80,000 | 10,000 units |
| Spare / programming | FarDriver jigs, pack BT fixtures | $20,000 | 10,000 units |
| **Total** | | **$160,000** | |

Per-unit surcharge at 10,000 units ≈ **$16**. After Year 1, fixtures are paid.

### 2. Explicitly withdrawn

* $120,000 carbon-steel **extrusion dies**
* $180,000 **hinge stamps** as SkeletKey-owned mill tooling
* $42/unit die surcharge and die-ownership clauses aimed at a frame factory

If a **frame OEM** has its own tooling, that cost sits in the **frame purchase price**, not our die book.

### 3. Ownership

Jigs and test equipment are SkeletKey’s. They can move to a second assembler. Personal guarantees omitted on equipment lines where possible.

# Preliminary Tooling Amortization Schedule
**Project:** Folding Electric Motorcycle Scale-Up (10,000 Units/Year)
**Component Focus:** Custom carbon steel Aluminum Extrusion Dies & Structural Stamping Tooling

## 1. Executive Summary
This amortization schedule outlines the financial mechanism to absorb **$300,000.00** of up-front non-recurring engineering (NRE) tooling costs directly into the per-unit Bill of Materials (BOM). By structuring a tooling amortization agreement with the Tier 1/Tier 2 manufacturer, initial CapEx is deferred and repaid on a piece-rate basis over the first contract volume milestones.

## 3. Quarterly Cash Flow Repayment Schedule
Assuming a ramp schedule to hit 10,000 units in Year 1:
* **Q1 (1,500 units):** $63,000.00 repaid ($36,000.00 Dies / $27,000.00 Stamping)
* **Q2 (2,500 units):** $105,000.00 repaid ($60,000.00 Dies / $45,000.00 Stamping) -> *Extrusion Dies fully paid off at Unit 5,000.*
* **Q3 (3,000 units):** $54,000.00 repaid ($0.00 Dies / $54,000.00 Stamping) -> *BOM cost drops by $24.00 per unit.*
* **Q4 (3,000 units):** $54,000.00 repaid ($0.00 Dies / $54,000.00 Stamping) -> *Stamping Tooling fully paid off at Unit 10,000.*

## 4. Contractual Safeguards & Performance Clauses
* **Tooling Ownership:** Intellectual property (IP) and physical ownership of the extrusion dies transfer completely to the brand once the amortization volume threshold is satisfied.
* **Early Buyout Clause:** The brand retains the right to buy out the remaining tooling balance at any time with zero interest penalties to transfer the physical molds to an alternative manufacturing facility.
* **Defect Liability:** Tooling wear, maintenance, and regular calibration costs remain the financial responsibility of the contract manufacturer during the amortization window.

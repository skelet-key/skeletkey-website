# Contract Manufacturer Due Diligence Checklist
## Facility Site Visit Protocol

### 1. Structural & Metal Fabrication Audit (carbon steel Frame Track)
*   [ ] **Post-Weld Heat Treatment Equipment:** Verify the presence of operational aging ovens capable of restoring structural properties to the carbon steel chassis after welding.
*   [ ] **Non-Destructive Testing (NDT):** Confirm the factory floor features active ultrasonic or X-ray inspection bays calibrated specifically for validating carbon steel alloy integrity.
*   [ ] **Tooling & Die Storage:** Inspect the environmental controls of the storage area where your custom $120,000.00 extrusion dies will be held to prevent corrosion.

### 2. Powertrain Assembly & High-Voltage Isolation
*   [ ] **ESD-Protected Areas (EPA):** Ensure the battery marriage line features ground-connected flooring and active anti-static wrist-strap monitoring for technicians.
*   [ ] **Inline Insulation Testers:** Locate the automated equipment assigned to run dielectric breakdown testing on your 16S2P configuration layout.
*   [ ] **Thermal Cycling Chambers:** Confirm the presence of environmental test rooms capable of running active burn-in cycles on assembled power packs.

### 3. Final Assembly & End-of-Line Verification
*   [ ] **Dynamometer Test Track:** Physically verify the existence of an automated dynamometer station equipped to log motor hall sensor feedback and braking regeneration curves.
*   [ ] **Water Spray Testing Booth:** Inspect the high-pressure water spray enclosure to guarantee it can accurately replicate environmental sealing standards for vehicle waterproofing.

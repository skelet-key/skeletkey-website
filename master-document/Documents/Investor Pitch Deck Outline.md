# INVESTOR PITCH DECK OUTLINE
## Puca by SkeletKey — sourced parts, contract assembly, 10,000/year
## Updated: 2026-08-21

### SLIDE 1: THE VEHICLE
* Puca: folding electric motorcycle for cities and travel.
* **$6,999** MSRP · ~70 mph class · 100+ miles at 45 mph · modular ~60 lb pack.
* Frame folds; pack slides out; 9.2 lb sourced steel chassis + 28.6 lb QS205 hub.

### SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM
* Long-range electrics are expensive, don’t fold, don’t drop the battery, don’t fly/train easily.
* Traditional OEM path burns capital on **frame factories**. We refuse that trap.

### SLIDE 3: HOW WE BUILD (THIS IS THE BUSINESS)
* **We source frames and parts.** We **do not** mill or robot-weld a SkeletKey chassis plant.
* **Contract assemblers** kit our consigned BOM to **10,000 units/year** (~40/day).
* Labor target **$500/unit**. No 10–15% factory parts markup.

### SLIDE 4: HARDWARE THAT ALREADY EXISTS
* **HAVE:** QS205 V3 50H 3T motor; FarDriver ND72360 BT; CCS1 inlet + Küster lock.
* **IN TRANSIT:** sourced folding frame, 190 mm dropout.
* **Pack:** ~8.5 kWh semi-solid NMC+, ~63 V, ~60 lb, dual QS8, slide-in.

### SLIDE 5: QUALITY WITHOUT A WELD SHOP
* IQC on **vendor frames and packs**.
* Line hipot / lock / flash.
* EOL roll test + spray on seals (IP65).

### SLIDE 6: UNIT ECONOMICS
* MSRP $6,999 · BOM $3,200 · assembly $500 · gross **~$3,299 (47%)**.
* 10,000 units → ~$70M revenue · ~$33M gross.

### SLIDE 7: CAPITAL USE
* Inventory of **kits** (frames, packs, motors) — not $300k extrusion dies.
* Assembly/test NRE ~$160k.
* Financing against inventory, **no personal guarantees**.

### SLIDE 8: THE ASK
* Working capital for long-lead **packs and frames**.
* Assembler capacity reservation.
* Certification (motorcycle class, UN38.3, CCS path).

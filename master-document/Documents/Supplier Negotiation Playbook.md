# Supplier Negotiation Playbook
## Program: 10,000-Unit Folding Electric Motorcycle Run

### 1. Counter-Strategy: The "Material Markup" Pushback
*   **Factory Stance:** The factory claims a 100% consigned model is unprofitable and demands a 5% to 10% "handling fee" on your pre-sourced components.
*   **Your Pivot:** Hold firm on the $500.00 assembly labor ceiling. Offer a tiered material handling incentive capped at a flat $15.00 per unit, structured strictly as a bonus paid only if the factory hits a 99.5% First Time Through (FTT) QA rate for that month.

### 2. Counter-Strategy: The "Tooling Amortization" Risk
*   **Factory Stance:** The manufacturer refuses to fund or defer the $300,000.00 up-front tooling cost for your custom carbon steel extrusion dies.
*   **Your Pivot:** Agree to pay 30% of the tooling upfront ($90,000.00) using a dedicated equipment credit line that omits personal guarantees. Amortize the remaining 70% ($210,000.00) over the first 5,000 units. Secure a legal clause stating that if production is terminated early by the factory, ownership of the carbon steel dies transfers immediately to you without further payout.

### 3. Counter-Strategy: The "Capacity Reservation" Retainer
*   **Factory Stance:** The Tier 1 CM demands a rolling 3-month cash deposit to reserve floor space for your 40 units-per-day production cadence.
*   **Your Pivot:** Reject multi-month cash deposits that drain working capital. Counter with a Letter of Credit (LC) backed by your inventory financing facility. This guarantees payment upon delivery of finished, QA-passed motorcycles while preserving daily operational liquidity.

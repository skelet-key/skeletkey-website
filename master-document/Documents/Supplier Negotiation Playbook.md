# Supplier Negotiation Playbook
## Program: 5,000-unit Puca — sourced parts, contract assembly
## Updated: 2026-08-21

### 1. Assembler wants a parts markup
* **They say:** Consigned kits are unprofitable; they want 5–10% on our parts.
* **We say:** **$500/unit** all-in labor/OH/QA. Optional **$15/unit** handling bonus only if monthly FTT ≥ 99.5%. We already bought the frame, motor, pack.

### 2. Assembler wants to “tool the frame”
* **They say:** Pay $300k for extrusion dies / hinge stamps.
* **We say:** **Withdrawn.** Frames are **sourced complete**. We will fund **assembly jigs and EOL test** (~$160k, SkeletKey-owned), not a mill.

### 3. Frame OEM (separate negotiation)
* Buy complete folders: **190 mm** dropout, stated mass, 44 mm headset class, stay tabs for deck.
* Dual-source. Quality is incoming inspection + OEM certs, not our weld cell.

### 4. Capacity retainer
* Reject 3-month cash deposits. Use LC / pay-on-QA-passed units against inventory financing. No personal guarantees.

### 5. Pack OEM
* Hold **~8.5 kWh / ~63 V / ~60 lb / dual QS8 / 6–8 kW** as gates. Bluetooth BMS required.

### 1. Unit economics (5,000 units)

| Metric | Per unit | Annualized |
|--------|----------|------------|
| MSRP | **$6,999** | $34,995,000 |
| Sourced BOM | $2,889 | $14,445,000 |
| Contract assembly & QA | $500 | $2,500,000 |
| **All-in COGS** | **$3,389** | **$16,945,000** |
| Gross margin | **$3,610 (52%)** | **$18,050,000** |

We **buy** frames and parts in **5,000-unit lots**. All-in is **$3,389** (parts **$2,889** + assembly **$500**). Frame **$375 includes rear shock**. Pack **$1,200**. FarDriver factory lot **$150** (paid $199 for one).

### 2. Where margin is spent

- Ocean freight and 3PL for **finished bikes and spare packs** (Class 9 for modules).
- Dual-source buffers on **frames, cells/packs, motors**.
- DOT / motorcycle classification, UN38.3 on the pack, CCS/electrical safety.
- Inventory financing against **kits and finished goods**, no personal guarantees.

### 3. What we are *not* funding as the scale plan

- A SkeletKey-owned frame factory.
- $300k carbon-steel **extrusion dies** as a gate to 5k units.
- Raw 6061-T6 **profile mill** contracts for the chassis (6061 is deck/enclosure only).

### 4. Cash timing

Long-lead items: **packs, frames, motors**. Assembler labor is largely **pay-on-output**. Working capital is inventory of *parts*, not a weld shop WIP.

# SEATING INSTALLATION
## Two-seater café racer seat on the Puca rear deck
## Document Reference: SEAT-2026-V1
## Updated: 2026-08-21

Puca does **not** use a seatpost. The café-racer seat sits **above the ~60 lb slide-in pack** on a **6061 aluminum deck** bolted to the sourced carbon-steel folding frame’s stay tabs.

### Exploded view

| # | Part | Role |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | Seat pan / base plate | Fiberglass or aluminum |
| 5 | Mounting bolt (typ.) | Hex bolt + washer; use thread locker |
| 6 | Rubber isolator / bushing | Isolates vibration; prevents metal-to-metal |
| 7 | Subframe | Tubular steel rear subframe |
| 8 | Frame rails | Upper rails to main frame |
| 9 | Rear seat loop | Structure and rear mount point |

**Mounting summary**

* **Front:** Bolted under tank or to upper frame rail.
* **Rear:** Bolt through rubber isolator / bushing into subframe loop or seat stay.
* **Side:** Brackets for lateral support and alignment.

**Notes:** Check tightness after install. Rubber isolators cut vibration and noise. Thread locker on mounting bolts.

## 3. Puca-specific stack (production / prototype)

This is the same principle, adapted to the **battery deck** (no tank rear, no seatpost):

```
  [café-racer two-up or solo pan]
           |
  [seat rails + rubber isolators]
           |
  [~60 lb pack 19–20″ × 6.5″ × 4.5–5″]
           |
  [6061 deck ~20″ × 8″]
           |
  [tube legs → bolt to stay tabs on sourced steel frame]
```

* TIG the **aluminum** deck/rails as a sub-assembly.
* **Bolt only** to the carbon-steel folder — no aluminum-to-steel weld.
* Rear load path is **seat pan → isolators → deck → stay tabs**, not the folding hinge.
* Leave left side open for QS205 disc + cables.
* Pack must still **slide in/out** with the seat on; if the pan blocks the lock, use a hinged or removable pan.

### Hardware (typical)

* Rubber isolators / bushings at every pan-to-deck bolt.
* Grade 8 (or 8.8) bolts + nylocks + thread locker.
* Optional side brackets to deck walls for two-up length.

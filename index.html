Battery Technology

57 lb lock-in modular pack. 350 Wh/kg semi-solid NMC+.

The heart of Puca is a 57-pound lock-in modular battery — not a fixed brick. We have already secured a long-term supply contract for semi-solid NMC+ cells at 350 Wh/kg, delivering exceptional energy density so the pack stays light enough to remove, ship, and swap while still supporting 100+ miles of real-world range at 45 mph and 8 kW CCS1-compatible fast charge — about 20% → 80% in 30 minutes.

Large-format NMC pouch cells and modular pack assembly — the energy core of SkeletKey

350 Wh/kg Semi-Solid NMC+ — Under Contract

Large-format semi-solid NMC+ pouch cells at 350 Wh/kg in a 16S2P layout deliver a nominal 60.8V pack with approximately 8.5 kWh of usable energy. A long-term supply contract is already in place for this chemistry — locking in the density that makes a true long-range pack possible at only 57 pounds. Integrated Bluetooth BMS app control reports cell health, state of charge, and faults live.

8 kW Fast Charge — CCS1 Worldwide

Charge at up to 8,000 watts on the growing global CCS1 fast-charge network — or use an ultra-compatible Tesla charger path with a CCS1 adapter where supported. Typical session: roughly 20% to 80% in about 30 minutes at 8 kW, so you’re back on the road without a long layover. Pair with the Bluetooth BMS app to monitor charge rate, temperature, and limits in real time.

Isolated Exoskeleton Cradle

To protect the central folding hinge, the battery never sits inside the frame cavity. It rides in an isolated structural exoskeleton cradle on the non-folding rear triangle — between the seat and the rear carriage.

Modular Lock-In Mechanism — 57 lb

This is not a loosely seated tray. Two thin 6061-T6 aluminum guide rails form the cradle sides. The 57 lb module slides in and locks until positive and negative contacts mate with quick-connect terminals. Dual over-center cam latches secure it — mechanical retention and electrical connection in one motion. Rigid at 70 mph. Off in seconds when you fold, store, or ship the pack separately.

6061-T6 Frame Contract

Parallel to the battery program, we have established a long-term 6061-T6 folding frame contract: heat-treated aluminum with approximately 2× the strength of traditional 6061, dropout geometry matched to the hub motor, and customizable frame length so production stays aligned with Puca’s ride and fold geometry.

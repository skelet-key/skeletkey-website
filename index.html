Battery Technology

59 lb lock-in modular pack. 350 Wh/kg semi-solid NMC+ cutting-edge energy dense batteries.

The heart of Puca is a 59-pound lock-in modular battery — not a fixed brick. We have already secured a long-term supply contract for 350 Wh/kg semi-solid NMC+ cutting-edge energy dense batteries, delivering exceptional energy density so the pack stays light enough to remove, ship, and swap.

That density still supports 100+ miles of real-world range at 45 mph and 8 kW CCS1-compatible fast charge — about 20% → 80% in 45 minutes.

350 Wh/kg Semi-Solid NMC+ — Cutting-Edge Energy Dense

Large-format semi-solid NMC+ pouch cells at 350 Wh/kg — cutting-edge energy dense batteries — in a 16S2P layout deliver a nominal 63V pack with approximately 8.5 kWh of usable energy.

A long-term supply contract is already in place for this chemistry — locking in the density that makes a true long-range pack possible at only 59 pounds.

Integrated Bluetooth BMS app control reports cell health, state of charge, and faults live.

8 kW Fast Charge — CCS1 Worldwide

Charge at up to 8,000 watts on the growing global CCS1 fast-charge network — or use an ultra-compatible Tesla charger path with a CCS1 adapter where supported.

Typical session: roughly 20% to 80% in 45 minutes at 8 kW, so you’re back on the road without a long layover.

Pair with the Bluetooth BMS app to monitor charge rate, temperature, and limits in real time.

Isolated Exoskeleton Cradle

To protect the central folding hinge, the battery never sits inside the frame cavity. It rides in an isolated structural exoskeleton cradle on the non-folding rear triangle — between the seat and the rear carriage.

Modular Lock-In Mechanism — 59 lb

This is not a loosely seated tray. Two thin carbon steel aluminum guide rails form the cradle sides. The 59 lb module slides in and locks until positive and negative contacts mate with quick-connect terminals.

Dual over-center cam latches secure it — mechanical retention and electrical connection in one motion. Rigid at 70 mph. Off in seconds when you fold, store, or ship the pack separately.

Carbon Steel Frame Contract

Parallel to the battery program, the production folding frame is carbon steel at 9.2 lb, with dropout geometry matched to the hub motor.

The measured frame weight is 9.2 lb; the hub motor is 28.6 lb. Geometry stays aligned with Puca’s ride and fold requirements.

Maintainability

Built to open, not to lock you out.

SkeletKey ships with complete documentation and an easy-to-read wiring chart covering turn signals, brake lights, throttle, ignition, cutoff switch, and headlamp — all accessible through the dashboard app on the phone that magnetically docks to the bike.

Full documentation & wiring chart

Every electrical path is documented. Turn signals, brake lights, throttle, ignition, cutoff switch, and headlamp are mapped in a clear chart you can follow from the app or the printed guide — so diagnosis and upgrades don’t require guesswork.

Wireless transmitter, cleaner fold

A reliable, waterproof wireless transmitter replaces the old tangle of multi-foot harnesses across the folding joint. Signals for controls and lighting cross the hinge without a thick cable bundle — keeping the chassis cleaner and the fold simpler.

Numbered connectors, easy repair

Transmitter and receiver boards use a simple diagram of numbered connectors. Swap or modify a wirelessly communicated component by matching numbers — no tracing five-foot runs of wire through the frame.

Reliable and latency-free

The wireless link is designed to be reliable and latency-free under real riding conditions. What used to be a messy harness becomes modular boards you can service, replace, or extend with confidence.

History

Iterating until the fold was light enough to matter.

Before the production plan, we built and rebuilt. Each generation of the Puca prototype taught us what to keep — and what to strip away.

Design iteration one

First complete machines proved the folding concept and long-range battery could coexist — but weight and complexity were still too high for real apartment and travel use.

Design iteration two

Those lessons became SkeletKey — focused on foldability, modular power, and the lowest practical chassis weight so the bike fits elevators, trunks, and real city life.

Story

From Amsterdam folding bikes to a motorcycle that travels.

The idea for SkeletKey was conceived in Amsterdam, inspired by the city’s culture of folding e-bikes — machines that disappear into apartments, trains, and daily life. The question was simple: why shouldn’t a real electric motorcycle fold the same way?

While building the first prototypes and waiting on my Dutch wife’s K-1 visa to be approved, I traveled with the frame four times back and forth. The bike had to move as easily as we did.

We’re now happily married for over two years and living in New York City. SkeletKey is the machine that came out of that journey — a folding, modular, long-range electric motorcycle built for people who refuse to choose between performance and portability.
